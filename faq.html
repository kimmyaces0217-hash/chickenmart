FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Quick answers about chicken, halal assurance, delivery, branches and ordering.

Is your chicken halal certified?

Yes. Favor Farm Fresh Chicken is presented as a halal chicken business. Keep the official halal certification documentation and badge details current on the live website.

Do you deliver anywhere in Nairobi?

We serve many Nairobi neighbourhoods. Delivery availability and charges can depend on location and order size.

What are your delivery charges?

Delivery charges depend on your location and order size. WhatsApp us for an exact quote.

Can I make a bulk order?

Yes. We accept household, restaurant, hotel and other bulk orders.

How do I place an order?

Go to Products, add the parts and quantities you want, then click Send Order on WhatsApp.

Can I order specific chicken parts?

Yes. The catalogue is designed around individual chicken parts so customers can order only what they need.

Can I order for a restaurant or hotel?

Yes. Contact us to discuss recurring supply, bulk quantities and delivery arrangements.

How do I find my nearest branch?

Visit Areas We Serve for the current branch list or WhatsApp us with your location.
