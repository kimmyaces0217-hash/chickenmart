ABOUT US

Fresh. Trusted. Halal.

Our mission is to make fresh, high-quality chicken parts convenient, reliable, and accessible to families, restaurants, and businesses across Nairobi. OUR STORY

Quality Chicken.
Trusted by Many.

Chicken Mart is a Nairobi-based poultry business dedicated to providing fresh, high-quality chicken parts to our customers. We source our chicken carefully and prepare our products to meet high standards of quality and safety.

We believe everyone deserves convenient access to clean, fresh and reliable chicken. With multiple branches across Nairobi and a dedicated delivery service, we bring freshness and convenience right to your doorstep.
